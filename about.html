A little about us

Helping pets and people thrive together.

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a pet care and training academy concept based in Durban, created to make learning about animal care clear, welcoming and accessible.

Our purpose

Learning should feel supportive, practical and positive.

Our proposed courses help people learn how to care for, understand and train their pets. The website brings course information and quotation requests together in one convenient place.

We aim to offer a friendly, professional experience, with straightforward information and a consistent visual identity across digital platforms.
